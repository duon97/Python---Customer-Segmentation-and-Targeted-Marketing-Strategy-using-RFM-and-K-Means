## Customer Segmentation & Strategy

**Problem:**  
The company wants to boost business by applying different strategies to distinct customer groups.

**Approach:**  
- Collected, cleaned, and preprocessed transaction data.  
- Calculated Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics.  
- Applied K-means clustering to segment customers into meaningful groups.

**Key Insights:**  
- **High-Value Customers:** Frequent, recent, high spenders.  
- **Medium-Value Customers:** Moderate activity; potential for upselling.  
- **Low-Value Customers:** Inactive or low spenders; need engagement campaigns.

**Recommended Actions:**  
- Reward and retain high-value customers.  
- Upsell to medium-value customers.  
- Implement engagement campaigns to encourage repeat purchases from low-value customers.
